DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT PROCESS

How each app takes shape

Every project begins with one clearly defined user need. I reduce the idea to its essential interaction, prototype the main flow and test whether it feels understandable on a real phone. Visual design, game mechanics and technical decisions then grow around that core instead of adding complexity without purpose.

Focused product design

Navigation, controls and feedback are designed for small screens and short mobile sessions. Each release is reviewed for readable typography, clear actions, responsive layouts and consistent Turkish and English terminology.

Testing and improvement

Gameplay balance, GPS behavior, loading speed and device compatibility are tested according to the needs of each app. Store feedback and support messages help identify practical improvements for later updates.

Privacy and support

Every published product includes a dedicated privacy page and a direct support route. Store listings explain platform availability and important product details so visitors can make an informed choice before downloading.
