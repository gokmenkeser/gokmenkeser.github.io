Equal Cut

Precision Slice Puzzle

Equal Cut is a precision puzzle game built around one simple challenge: divide each shape as accurately as possible.

  • Slice different shapes into target proportions.
  • Aim for the perfect 50/50 cut.
  • Complete increasingly challenging levels.
  • Compare your precision and improve your score.
